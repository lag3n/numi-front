Obtaining Successful Results

We're still working on the accuracy of our program. Here are some tips for getting a better result:

  • Try to take the photo as still as possible. Blurriness caused by camera shift may obstruct our program from running successfully.
  • Take the photo against an uniformly coloured background, white if possible.
  • Ensure that the obverse and reverse photos are as flat as possible (don't take photos on an angle - the rims and shadows may obstruct the program).
  • Our program is currently unable to process coins that are heavily irregular in shape or coins that are in third-party slabs
